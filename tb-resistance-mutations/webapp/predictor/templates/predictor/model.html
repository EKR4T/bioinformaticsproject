Why balanced accuracy, not accuracy

For most drugs resistant isolates are rare (under 2% for the four newer drugs). A model that always says "susceptible" would score 98–99% plain accuracy but has a balanced accuracy of 0.50. Balanced accuracy is the mean of sensitivity and specificity, so it cannot be inflated that way.

Limits

  • Trained on CRyPTIC isolates, which over-sample drug-resistant TB; not validated on Kenyan or other African isolates.
  • Part of the signal for ethambutol, the fluoroquinolones and bedaquiline is co-resistance: mutations such as katG S315T mark MDR strains.
  • Mutations carried by fewer than 5 isolates, synonymous mutations and genes outside the 43 candidates are ignored.
  • Not a diagnostic. Use laboratory drug-susceptibility testing for treatment decisions.
